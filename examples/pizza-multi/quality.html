error Wrong kind of range (1) OB024

Datatype properties must have a datatype range and object properties a class range (OOPS! P31-like).

warning Term without a definition (5) OB011

Every term should have a definition (skos:definition, IAO:0000115 or rdfs:comment) (OOPS! P08).

  • Class “Meat topping” has no definition. §
  • Datatype property “Pizza” has no definition. §
  • Object property “has base” has no definition. §
  • Datatype property “has calories” has no definition. §
  • Object property “is topping of” has no definition. §
warning Duplicate labels (1) OB016

Two or more terms share the same label, which confuses readers and tools.

  • Label “pizza” @en is used by pizza:Name, pizza:Pizza. §
info Property without domain (2) OB012

Consider declaring rdfs:domain (OOPS! P11). Ignored when the property is used in class axioms.

  • Property “Pizza” has no domain. §
  • Property “has ingredient” has no domain. §
info Property without range (1) OB013

Consider declaring rdfs:range (OOPS! P11).

  • Property “has ingredient” has no range. §
info Deprecated term without replacement (1) OB018

Deprecated terms should point to their replacement with dcterms:isReplacedBy.

  • “Cheese topping” is deprecated but has no replacement. §
info Inconsistent naming convention (1) OB019

Classes are usually UpperCamelCase and properties lowerCamelCase (OOPS! P22).

  • Property name Name is not lowerCamelCase. §
