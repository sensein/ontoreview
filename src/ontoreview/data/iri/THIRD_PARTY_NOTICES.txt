THIRD-PARTY ONTOLOGY SNAPSHOTS

This SQLite database contains modified, extracted indexes of public W3C vocabulary
RDF documents: PROV-O, SOSA, SSN, OWL-Time, SKOS, ORG, DCAT, DQV, ODRL and RDF Data Cube.
Changes: RDF statements were reduced to typed-subject IRIs, types, plain-text labels,
deprecation flags and source metadata. This is not an official W3C distribution.
Original rights remain with the respective authors and copyright holders.

The adjacent top_terms.manifest.json identifies each original title, publisher
specification, downloaded source, timestamp, SHA-256, and declared license/rights.
